## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Numbered chapter titles + progress pills** (01 · 02 · 03) for list videos ("3 websites", "3 settings") — viewers know how many are left.
- **Open loop**: a small "revealed at the end" card early on, paid off (crossed out → reveal) at the end. Use sparingly and honestly.
- **Custom UI mock-ups that act out the idea** (toggle flips, rows turn red, a bar cracks) instead of generic stock B-roll — strongest for explaining invisible tech concepts.
- **Alternate dark and light canvases** per chapter to signal a new section.
- **Recap chips** (✕ ✕ ✕ / ✓ ✓ ✓) to summarise before the payoff.
- **Callback ending**: return to the opening visual (phone mock-up) now "fixed".
- **Face bubble with side captions** when the graphic needs most of the screen.
