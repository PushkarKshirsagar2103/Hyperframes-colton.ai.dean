# @dhananjay_tech — Style Fingerprint

Rolled up across 3 videos. See `videos/*/analysis.md` for per-video detail. Last distilled: 2026-10-25.

## Signature moves (consistent across videos)
- Hook pattern: starts mid-thought with a concrete, valuable claim (a list of named products, a "99% / 2 problems" stat, or the result first: "So yes!") and a graphic lands within the first second.
- Caption treatment: varies by format — short phrase chunks with one highlighted keyword (reel 1), keyword titles only on demo-heavy reels (reel 2), small pill captions on a split seam in Roman-script Hinglish (reel 3). Consistent: few words on screen at once, one emphasised word.
- Transition rotation: plain jump cuts within A-roll; blur-through when switching between "face world" and "screen world"; one special beat per video (desaturate on the twist, flash into the payoff).
- Face treatment: centred medium shot, warm home-studio or bright set; face is never covered — graphics live in the top third or the top half of a split.
- Palette: magenta #E6197E (personal brand), highlighter yellow #FFD21F, red #E0221B for problems, green for positives, light paper #ECEBE8 / white cards in split mode.
- Energy profile: front-loaded and breathing — fast setup, then steady explanation, then a payoff that is allowed to hold.

## What to steal (ranked by Hyperframes copy-ability)
1. Split-screen explainer board (top canvas with clean white cards, face below) — `short-form-video` BOTTOM face mode.
2. Word-synced icon/logo pop-ins for spoken product names, timed from `transcript.json`.
3. Phone-in-hand demo footage with one tap indicator per tap.
4. Real screenshots as floating cards with one highlighted phrase.
5. Full-screen hold for the payoff/proof moment.
6. One deliberate "twist" beat per video (grade change or pause).

## Don't copy
- Magenta brand colour, scrapbook/torn-paper/handwritten styling, multi-colour loud palette
- English-translated captions over Hinglish speech (reel 1)
- Meme cutaways, "DID WHATT 🤯" phrasing, comment-bait wording, his studio sets
