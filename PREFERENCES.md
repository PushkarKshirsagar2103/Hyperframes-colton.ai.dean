## Guiding principle — flexible, not templated

- **The content decides the layout.** Pick full-screen talking head, split screen, full-screen B-roll, product footage, screen recording, or picture-in-picture per beat, based on what communicates that point best. Never force every video into the same layout or structure (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- The numbers in `EDITING_BLUEPRINT.md` (pause length, zoom count, caption length, timings) are **guides, not rules**. Use judgement; the feel (premium, natural, clear) wins over the number (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- No element is mandatory: not every video needs music, a progress tracker, chapter cards, a twist beat, or an outro (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- Learn from the reference creators (`_reference/creator-library/`) but never copy any one creator exactly — no scripts, footage, music, graphics or branded looks (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)

## Visual style

_(colors, typography, density, placement — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Premium, clean, modern, minimalist — Apple-like. Generous whitespace, restrained palette, crisp typography (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- Natural and engaging, not overly polished or ad-like — it should feel like a creator talking, not a commercial (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- Motion graphics only when they help explain or emphasize something (specs, comparisons, steps, a key number) — never as decoration (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- This minimalist brief overrides the busier `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md` texture defaults (perspective grid, crosshairs, grain, chrome text) — keep its discipline (one idea per beat, clean transitions), drop the clutter (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- **Font: Inter** only — ExtraBold for titles/numbers, Bold for captions, Medium for card text, tracked small caps for labels (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- **Both dark and light layouts**: near-black `#0B0B0D` is the primary background; the light panel `#F2F2F0` is used when it suits the content (e.g. a bright explainer board or a section change). Both must stay premium and clean (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- Electric lime `#C8FF2E` is an accent only — never a large fill. Red/green only as small ✕/✓ markers in comparisons (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- **Split-screen explainer board** (graphic canvas on top, face below) is the go-to layout for explanations and comparisons — when it suits the content, not by default for every video (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 2)
- **Proof over decoration**: show real screenshots, spec sheets, product footage and screen recordings as clean cards with one highlighted line; give a genuine payoff (result, benchmark, before/after) the full screen and let it hold (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 4)
- **List videos** may use a numbered progress tracker and short chapter cards when it helps viewers follow — optional, not every list needs them (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 3)
- Graphics and captions never cover the face; leave the footage's colour natural (gentle contrast, warm skin) (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)

## Pacing

_(scene length, cuts vs. transitions, breathing room — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Remove long pauses, dead air, and bad/repeated takes — but keep natural breathing room so the delivery stays human, not jump-cut-every-word (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- Keep edits simple — avoid overcomplicated sequences (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- **Cutting guide**: remove dead air, long awkward pauses, false starts and repeated takes. ~0.4s is a *guide* for "too long a pause", not an absolute rule; keep natural breaths (~0.2s), conversational rhythm and meaningful fillers ("matlab", "toh", "yaar"). Never make the delivery sound rushed or robotic (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 7)
- **Never change the meaning** of what Pushkar said, and never rearrange dialogue in a way that misrepresents it (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- Payoffs and endings are allowed to breathe — don't cut them short (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)

## Captions

_(style, color, sync tightness, word-by-word vs. phrase chunks — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Bold, highly readable captions in white with a tasteful electric-lime accent on key words (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- Captions must handle Hinglish — transcribe what was actually said (Hindi words in Roman script as spoken), never "translate" or rewrite the speech (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- **Karaoke reveal**: phrase shows in grey `#8E8E93`, each word turns white as spoken; Inter Bold; soft shadow, no stroke or box (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 1)
- **Selective lime**: at most one lime word per phrase, only on a word that carries meaning (number, product, verdict); many phrases get none (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 1)
- **Phrase length varies naturally** with the speech — short phrases are typical (roughly 2–4 words) but not enforced (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- **Accuracy gate**: if transcription is uncertain (Hinglish often is on this machine), show Pushkar the caption lines for correction **before rendering** (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- A script or rough notes from Pushkar is **supporting material only** — never assume it matches the recorded delivery word for word; captions follow the recording (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)

## Transitions

_(preferred / avoid — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Clean, simple transitions — vary them; never repeat the same transition back-to-back across a video (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- Avoid excessive zooms (punch-ins sparingly, only for emphasis) (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- **Restrained, chosen per scene**: hard cuts within talking-head/B-roll and soft blur/dissolve between face and screen are the usual choices; pick what suits the scene. This overrides `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s whip-streak / "never hard cuts" defaults (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 5)
- **Subtle zooms used sparingly** — no fixed quota; only for genuine emphasis (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)

## Audio

_(music vibe, voiceover style, SFX, mix — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Preserve Pushkar's original voice — no voice replacement or TTS over their speech (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- SFX only when they genuinely add something; default to fewer (2026-10-25, stated at setup)
- **Music is optional** — use it only when it improves the video; the voice must always stay clear. When used, sit it roughly 18–22 dB under the voice and duck under speech (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 6)
- **Music licensing**: use a track only if its licence and suitability are clear; otherwise leave music out and ask Pushkar for an approved track. The bundled `assets/music/music-bed-cinematic-tech.mp3` has no recorded title/author/source link, so it is **not cleared** for use without Pushkar's OK (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- **SFX sparingly**, only where appropriate — no fixed count (2026-10-25, approved blueprint — addition 6)

## Endings & reveals

- **Choose the ending from the content**: a clean payoff, a closing line, a platform-specific follow line, or a return to the opening shot — whichever fits (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
- Reveals / "at the end" teasers are occasionally welcome when they genuinely improve the story; never manufacture suspense (2026-10-25, approved blueprint)
