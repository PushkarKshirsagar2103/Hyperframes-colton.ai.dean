## 3. The POV Pushkar blueprint

**One sentence:** a calm, premium talking-head video where you stay the anchor, captions read like clean Apple-style subtitles in your own Hinglish, and the screen only changes to *show* what you're saying: the product, the screen or the number.

### 3.1 Video structure (default 30–45s; reviews/comparisons up to ~60s)

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–2s | Start mid-thought on the most valuable line (the result, a surprising number, or "X vs Y"). One visual lands by ~0.5s: the product shot, a frosted number card, or a logo pop. No intro, no greeting. |
| **Promise** | 2–5s | Say what they'll get ("3 websites", "battery test result end mein"). For list videos, show the step tracker here. |
| **Body** | 5s → end−6s | One idea per beat. Each beat gets the visual that proves it (product B-roll, screen recording, spec card, comparison card). Return to full face between beats so it stays personal. |
| **Payoff** | ~3–6s | The result gets the full screen: benchmark number, before/after, final demo. Hold it, and keep everything else still. |
| **Outro** | last 2–4s | One closing line on a softly blurred background **or** a simple follow line with the correct handle (@povpushkar on Reels / Pov Pushkar on Shorts). No fake buttons. |

### 3.2 Three video formats

1. **Review / comparison:** face + product B-roll + split-screen spec cards. Comparisons use a two-column card (A | B) with lime only on the winner. End on the verdict at full screen.
2. **Tutorial / tech tip / useful website:** step tracker on top + screen recordings in a clean device frame (or phone-in-hand footage) + one tap/click ring per action. Face as a bubble during long screen segments.
3. **AI tool / news / story:** split-screen explainer board with "proof" cards (screenshots, prompts, outputs, highlighted lines) + one full-screen payoff.

### 3.3 Face modes (use these three only)
- **Full face** (default): any personal or opinion line, the hook, the outro.
- **Split screen**: top ~45% canvas / bottom ~55% face, for explaining, specs and comparisons. A thin divider in white at 15% opacity. Lime is kept for emphasis only.
- **Face bubble**: a small circle bottom-left, only while a screen recording or demo needs the whole frame.
- Never cover the face with graphics or captions.

## 4. Guidelines

### Captions
- **Words:** exactly what you said, in **Roman-script Hinglish** (e.g. "yeh setting sabse important hai"), with English words in English. Never translated, never rewritten, never in Devanagari, filler words left as spoken. *(Brand/product names spelled correctly.)*
- **Style:** karaoke reveal. The phrase appears in grey (`#8E8E93` at ~70%) and each word turns **white** as it's spoken.
- **Accent:** at most **one lime word per phrase**, and only on a word that carries meaning (number, product, verdict). Many phrases get none.
- **Chunking:** 2–4 words per phrase, one line, never more than two lines.
- **Font:** a bold, clean sans (Inter Bold/ExtraBold) at ~64–72px on a 1080-wide frame. Soft shadow only: no stroke, no box. *(In split mode use a smaller, ~44px, dark pill caption on the seam, Reel 3 style.)*
- **Position:** ~68–72% down the frame in full-face mode, inside the Reels/Shorts safe zone (clear of the right-side buttons and bottom description). On the seam in split mode, beside the bubble in bubble mode.
- **Animation:** words fade/brighten in ~80–120ms. No bounce, no scale-pop on every word.

### Color (proposed `assets/brand-tokens.css`; needs your OK)
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--brand-bg` | `#0B0B0D` | Full-screen cards, dark canvas |
| `--brand-surface` | `#16161A` | Cards on dark |
| `--brand-surface-2` | `#F2F2F0` | Optional light canvas (split board), for variety by section |
| `--brand-border` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` | Card edges, divider |
| `--brand-accent` | `#C8FF2E` (electric lime) | One word per phrase, winner highlight, progress fill |
| `--brand-accent-glow` | `rgba(200,255,46,0.25)` | Very subtle glow on accent only |
| `--brand-warn` | `#FF453A` | Only for a "✕ / bad" state in comparisons |
| `--brand-text` | `#FFFFFF` | Captions, titles |
| `--brand-text-dim` | `#8E8E93` | Unspoken karaoke words, small labels |
- Lime is an accent, never a big background fill. Red/green only as small ✓/✕ markers, never as decoration.
- Leave the footage colour natural: gentle contrast, warm skin. No heavy filters.

### Typography
- **One family: Inter** (free, very close to Apple's SF). ExtraBold for titles and numbers, Bold for captions, Medium for card text, and tracked small caps (letter-spacing ~0.12em) for labels like "BATTERY" or "STEP 2".
- Big numbers are the hero on full-screen cards (~180–240px), with a small label above and a thin rule below (technolobe-style, in your colours).
- No serif and no handwritten/sticker fonts. *(A serif accent is possible if you want it later, but it's technolobe's signature, so it's not recommended.)*

### B-roll, product shots, screen recordings
- **Every cutaway must show what's being said at that moment.** No generic stock filler.
- **Product:** clean close-ups on a plain surface; slow push-ins or rotations at most 3–5% scale over the shot.
- **Screen recordings:** inside a simple device frame on the dark or light canvas, cropped tight to the relevant part. One lime ring or soft pointer per tap/click, timed to the tap. Speed up long waits (show a small "4x" pill).
- **Phone-in-hand demos** are fine and feel real (Reel 2). Keep them steady and in focus.
- **Shot length:** demos hold 2–5s so viewers can follow; quick product inserts 1–2s.
- **Logo/icon pop-ins** only when you name several apps/products in a row.

### Motion graphics (only when they explain)
- **Allowed kit (repeat it so the look stays consistent):**
  1. frosted info card (name · price · spec)
  2. full-screen number card (+ before → after strike-through)
  3. split-screen proof card with one highlighted line
  4. A | B comparison card
  5. step tracker + 1-second chapter card
  6. simple UI mock-up (toggle, checklist ✓, progress bar)
  7. tap/click ring
- **Motion:** 250–400ms ease-out entrances (fade + 8–12px rise or a 0.96→1 scale), the same exits. Nothing spins, bounces, shakes or shatters.
- **Zooms:** at most 2–3 subtle punch-ins (≤8%) per video, only on emphasis lines, and never two in a row.
- **Twist beat:** at most one per video (a brief dim or a held pause on "lekin…").

### Transitions
- Two flavors only: **hard cut** (within talking-head and B-roll) and **soft blur/dissolve** (~300ms) when switching between face and screen or into a full-screen card.
- Chapter cards in list videos act as the section transition.
- Never use whip pans, glitches, flashes, spins or shatter. *(This overrides `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s "never hard cuts" and whip-streak defaults for your brand.)*

### Sound
- **Your voice is the star:** cleaned (light noise reduction + gentle compression), never re-voiced.
- **Music bed:** one calm, modern track from `assets/music/` (or your own licensed track), sitting **~18–22 dB under the voice**. That's slightly quieter than the references (~10–15 dB), to keep it premium. Duck it under speech and let it rise slightly on the payoff/outro.
- **SFX:** 0–3 per video from `assets/sfx/`, only to mark a real moment (a soft click on a tap ring, a subtle whoosh into the payoff). Never on every caption or card.
- Target loudness around −14 LUFS (standard for Reels/Shorts).

### Pacing and cutting your footage
- **Remove:** pauses longer than ~0.4s, dead air, false starts, repeated takes (keep the best take), and "ek second" moments.
- **Keep:** a natural breath of ~0.15–0.25s between sentences, your rhythm and fillers that carry meaning ("matlab", "toh", "yaar"), and the meaning and order of what you said.
- **Visual rhythm:** something meaningful changes every ~2–4s (a cut, card, B-roll or layout change). Long face-only stretches get one gentle punch-in or a card, not constant motion.
- **Let it breathe:** the payoff holds 2–4s and the outro 2–4s.

### Avoid (from the references, explicitly)
Loud headline banners · giant multi-colour kinetic words · stamps, stickers, emoji, scrapbook textures · shatter, glitch, whip effects · translated captions · comment-bait gimmicks · fake buttons · busy palettes · more than one accent colour.

## 6. Limitations: what I could not verify

1. **I can't listen to audio.** The music-bed finding comes from measured audio levels (no silence anywhere; quietest moments 10–15 dB under the voice). Specific music, SFX hits and voice processing were **not verified**.
2. **Transcripts.** Only Reel 1 got a usable automatic transcript. Reel 2 came back **translated into English**, and Reel 3 came back in Devanagari and got stuck repeating words. Reels 4–6 weren't transcribed: one timed out, and the other two were stopped on your instruction. Spoken words for Reels 2–6 were read from their **on-screen captions**, which are verbatim Hinglish in Reels 3–6. Reel 2 has no running captions, so its exact wording isn't verified.
3. **⚠️ This matters for your own videos.** On this cloud computer, the fast speech model sometimes translates Hinglish to English or switches to Hindi script, and the more accurate model took over 10 minutes for a 27s clip and still didn't produce a transcript. So I **can't guarantee word-perfect Hinglish captions automatically**, and I can't check them by ear. Proposed fix: I generate the captions, then give you a plain text list of every caption line to approve or correct before anything is rendered. A quick script or bullet notes of what you said also helps a lot.
4. **Frames were reviewed at reduced size** (all frames at 2 per second, 8 per sheet). Fonts are identified by eye and colour hex values are estimates.
5. **Engagement data is partial.** Instagram exposed likes and comments but not views, and comment counts are inflated by comment-keyword CTAs. I made **no claims about which techniques perform better**.
6. **Small samples.** @technolobe has 1 video and @namansoni.ai has 2. The repo's rule is 3+ before writing a creator "fingerprint", so only @dhananjay_tech has one (`style-distilled.md`).
7. **Reel 5 appears to be a tool promotion.** Only its editing structure was used.
8. **Not yet set:** `assets/brand-tokens.css` is still blank (setup Step 4). The values in §4 are a proposal. The setup marker in `CREATOR.md` stays until you approve them.
